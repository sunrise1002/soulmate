# Code of conduct

Soulmate contributors must make participation respectful, safe, and focused on
constructive technical collaboration. Be considerate across languages, experience
levels, identities, and communication styles. Critique ideas and code without
attacking people; accept feedback in good faith; and protect personal or sensitive
information encountered during collaboration.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Do not include unnecessary personal data. Maintainers will limit disclosure to the
people needed to assess and resolve the report and will avoid conflicts of interest
where practical.
